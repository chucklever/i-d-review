# Meetings

This is the most logistics-heavy, least RFC-governed area of a
chair's work. RFC 2418 establishes that WGs conduct business at
meetings and, primarily, on the mailing list (RFC 2418 §3.1,
§2.2); the operational detail — session-request deadlines,
agenda cutoffs, interim-meeting policy, minute and recording
requirements — lives in IETF meeting policy and IESG
statements that change over time, not in a stable RFC. Pull the
current deadlines from the IETF meeting page or the datatracker
rather than quoting a fixed rule here.

## Sessions and interims

- Request a session for an upcoming IETF meeting through the
  datatracker before the published cutoff. Not every WG needs a
  session at every meeting; the mailing list is the primary
  venue and a session must earn its place with real agenda.
- Interim meetings are scheduled through the datatracker under
  the current IESG interim-meeting policy, with advance notice
  to the WG and the community.

## Agendas

- Publish the agenda to the list and datatracker ahead of the
  cutoff so participants can prepare. An agenda is a set of
  decisions or discussions the WG needs a room for, not a
  series of author status reports.
- Nothing is decided by virtue of being discussed in a room.
  Consensus is confirmed on the list (RFC 2418 §3.3); a
  meeting can surface a sense of the room, but the chair takes
  it back to the list to confirm.

## The record

- Minutes and an attendance record (blue sheets) are part of
  the WG's permanent record. Arrange a notetaker and, where
  used, a jabber/notes scribe before the session.
- Post minutes to the list and datatracker promptly, and give
  the WG a chance to correct them.
